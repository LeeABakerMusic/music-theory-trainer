Six interactive music games. Learn at your own pace with your MIDI keyboard or the on-screen piano.

New here? How to use Music Theory Trainer

Choose one of the six game tabs, select your settings, then press the green Start button. Use your connected MIDI keyboard or click the on-screen piano. For Key Signatures, choose an answer on screen.

Scales: practise major and minor scales or race through a timed Mega Run. Chords: learn inversions, take a timed challenge, or play unlimited random chords. Intervals, Ear Training, Sight-Reading and Key Signatures: relaxed learning games with clues and no time pressure.

To use a MIDI keyboard, scroll to the MIDI keyboard section, click Enable MIDI and choose your instrument. Changing game tabs ends the current attempt; saved records remain intact.

💡 Help me understand this interval

What is an interval? It is the distance between two notes. Each piano key you move to, including black keys, is one semitone. Two semitones make a tone.

Press Start to see how to work out your first interval.

Tip: you can hide the staff answer and note name using the checkboxes above, then use this explanation only when you need it.

Interactive piano

In Chord Challenge, click to hold and click again to release; use Release mouse notes to clear them all. In Scales, Intervals, Ear Training and Sight-Reading, keys play normally while pressed. The keyboard scrolls sideways on smaller screens.
